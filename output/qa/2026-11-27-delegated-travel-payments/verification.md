# Delegated payment for travel

27 November 2026, Hong Kong. This follow-up concerns the owner's requested interaction: the traveller approves a particular itinerary and complete fare in the conversation, then the assistant submits its payment and checks the booking. A second use case lets a traveller grant a limited mandate for later purchases.

## Etraveli’s role and test access

Booking Holdings says it began working with Etraveli in 2019 and extended the existing commercial agreement for eight years in 2025. Etraveli operates Flightnetwork, Gotogate and Mytrip. This establishes an identifiable flight-distribution business. Flightnetwork handles servicing for bookings through its MCP. In the EU consumer authorities’ flight-cancellation work, Etraveli was among the intermediaries that committed to passing on refunds within seven days of receiving them from the airline. A particular fare’s voluntary cancellation rights still depend on its terms. ## One supplier for the owner's card

The owner clarified that the funds must come from the traveller’s bank card. Their preferred interaction is one approval of the actual itinerary and total in chat, followed by an agent-submitted card purchase and a verified issued ticket. Consumer checkout inside the conversation is the fallback. The earlier Atlas replay used a seller balance and supplies evidence only for that workflow.

The engineering recommendation for Alice is **Nuitée Connect Flights**. Its flight booking endpoint documents immediate traveller-card processing through a separately enabled PCI endpoint. This establishes a potential processing path. A reusable traveller token, a single frictionless authorization with any particular issuer, and FYP’s entitlement remain unverified. Its standard payment SDK supports the fallback in the same product: client-side cardholder payment, then server-side flight booking and a separate retrieval of the booking detail for ticket data. Ancillary selection can replace the payment intent; the current total and the current intent must be reviewed together. A payment success without ticketing remains a distinct state. The account’s stored developer card and its credit line do not establish which traveller paid. [Flight booking and card modes](https://docs.nuitee.com/reference/post_flights-bookings) · [Payment and ticketing sequence](https://docs.nuitee.com/docs/flight-booking-architecture) · [Flight booking detail and ticket data](https://docs.nuitee.com/reference/get_flights-bookings).

The public registration page expressly offered a free account without a card. Flights sandbox access is enabled for new accounts, with limited synthetic inventory; production flight access requires separate activation. Examined the public registration form, official flight API and payment documentation, plus the existing FYP live checkout, charge and booking receipts. No external registration, application, account change or booking was made. The user selected the product outcome. Provider selection remains an engineering recommendation pending acceptance and verification. [Sandbox access](https://docs.nuitee.com/docs/getting-access-to-flights).
