# Flexible airport searches and reminders from an active conversation

Application `949d76df` is installed on the ordinary Mac Web, iPhone Simulator and original Pi. The operator's itinerary, supplier search identifiers, account details, and actual live fare amounts stay out of this public report. The screenshots use fictional data. [Acceptance measurements](checks.json).

## What failed

The original text audit contained a sandbox quote call with a city label and two IATA codes in its single destination field. Validation stopped before dispatch to the supplier. The chat surfaced a generic action error. Independently, the background researcher produced a reminder whose sole citation was the user's just-saved question. The limit on notification delivery did not protect the conversation's active finding list.

## What changed

Fresh flight searches use an owner-scoped `live_flight_search` Function in the existing OpenAI Session. Airport arguments can contain several physical IATA codes. The application accepts a clearly delimited label containing those codes, carries forward an explicit either/or answer, and expands the allowed routes into individual reads on the existing LetsFG connector. Compatible parallel calls can share a result list. Different dates, budgets, baggage requirements or unrelated route pairs cannot be merged. Explicit simulations continue to use the sandbox. [OpenAI Function tools](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/agents-api/tools/functions) · [Live flight contract](../../../desk-companion/docs/live-flight-agent.md).

Each accepted route retains its own supplier search ID and status. A pending route can be polled while completed results are displayed. A failed route leaves successful routes available. Each successful airport has a representative fare among the first choices. Additional options open on demand. A spoken-style numbered text selection or a tap reprices the chosen supplier offer. The existing checkout still requires an explicit versioned purchase confirmation with current terms. Published fares with missing baggage evidence keep their all-in totals unresolved.

The background researcher excludes the last 30 minutes of user statements from new research and checks citation eligibility before saving or delivering a finding. One chat statement cannot trigger its own reminder after that interval either. An independent mail, task or calendar change can still be actionable. Two settled statements can support a material conflict. A previously delivered conversational echo is available in the history view without occupying the ordinary transcript. Explicit snoozes, follow-up tasks and acknowledgements retain their original purpose.
