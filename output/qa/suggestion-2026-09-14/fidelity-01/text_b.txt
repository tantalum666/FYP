2. Problem Statement & Project Objectives
Instructions: Describe the business or technical problem in information technology that this project intends to address. Define 2-3 specific project objectives (what your solution intends to achieve for the target users/businesses).
Problem Statement:Buffet and batch-prepared self-service outlets need timely replenishment without excess food at closing. Visual checks can miss low stock or batches already being prepared, leading to waiting, duplicate work and leftovers. Customer menus can also show outdated availability.We propose continuous monitoring while trays remain in service, linking measurements, demand estimates and kitchen progress. Metafoodx and Controliza are commercial references; the prototype will be evaluated against simple replenishment baselines.Project Objectives:1. Implement continuous monitoring for up to three serving positions, recording food quantity, refills and surface temperature and flagging unreliable data.2. Develop AI-assisted replenishment recommendations using demand, preparation lead time and work in progress; compare empty-tray time and leftovers against fixed-threshold and consumption-rate baselines.3. Integrate bilingual staff tools and a supplementary QR menu/order interface; evaluate consistency, interruption recovery and usability.
3. Proposed Project Scope & Key Features
Instructions: Define the scope of your system. List and briefly describe the key modules, functions, or features that your group plans to implement. Projects must be implementation-oriented and student-centered.
Key Subsystems / Features:A. Continuous tray monitoring. Monitor weight, visual dish identity and surface temperature throughout service; handle tare, tray movement, refills, occlusion and stale data.B. Replenishment and kitchen tasks. Suggest timing and batch quantities using demand, preparation/closing times and work in progress. Staff confirm or adjust tasks and record completed refills.C. Operations dashboard and evaluation. Show alerts and export empty-tray time, refill history, leftovers and disposal/reuse records.D. Supplementary QR menu and ordering. Show dish details, availability, completed-order popularity or buffet take-up trends and estimated waits. Support fast-casual/made-to-order orders, checking pending demand against physical stock.Prototype Scope:Six months, one line, up to three positions and 4-6 registered dishes; excludes payments, delivery, procurement ERP and independent food-safety certification.Expected Users & Roles:Staff monitor and refill trays; managers maintain menus/settings and review reports; customers view menus and submit or track applicable orders.
