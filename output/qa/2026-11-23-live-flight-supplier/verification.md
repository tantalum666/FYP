# LetsFG live agent protocol and separate public sandbox control

The source adds real buyer-account OAuth, live roundtrip search, origin-bound offer rechecking, and an owner-only booking/status adapter. The original screenshot concerned one adult from Hong Kong to Tokyo Narita on 24 November 2026, returning 1 December. The published outbound was BR852 and BR196, 11:20–19:20 local, and HK$3,389.59 was the research fare. The screenshot did not verify a 20 kg checked bag or the complete payable amount. No live booking, hold, passenger transmission, or card charge was made.

The provider’s detailed agent guide supports a party in one call through the lead passenger and `additional_passengers`. Each seat is priced for the full itinerary. The shorter getting-started example describes a single lead traveller; the agent guide supplies the full-party contract. The standard live search schema does not promise a checked weight. The agent service has its own status POST keyed by the original supplier booking reference. The commercial Developer API, anonymous sandbox, and live OAuth paths have separate credentials and contracts. [Detailed agent contract](https://letsfg.co/for-agents) · [OAuth asset](https://letsfg.co/.well-known/oauth-protected-resource/mcp) · [Terms and supplier ancillaries](https://letsfg.co/en/terms).
