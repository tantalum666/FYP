## LetsFG payment methods

Search and developer-sandbox booking require no real card. The simulator returns synthetic payment and reservation information. For an actual passenger booking, a supported real card or the offered Revolut Pay option can be enrolled through a supplier-issued, account-specific page. The initial zero-amount enrolment creates an on-file method which remains available for later approved bookings until replaced or removed. Revolut handles payment details. LetsFG says it does not collect the full card number or security code. [Agent enrolment](https://letsfg.co/for-agents) · [Sandbox](https://letsfg.co/developers/docs/api-sandbox/index.html) · [Terms, sections 4 and 9](https://letsfg.co/en/terms).

For a personal assistant connection, sections 3 and 7 require the traveller to approve the particular trip on a LetsFG page before authorization of funds. A local Alice confirmation is a separate review. A higher price requires fresh approval, and the merchant captures funds when the supplier confirms. The terms describe business API approval separately. [Personal and business booking terms](https://letsfg.co/en/terms).
