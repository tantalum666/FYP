# Alice shopping on the iPhone 17 Pro Simulator

On 19 November 2026 the installed native app took a Chinese shopping request in Alice’s normal composer, quoted **2 × HK$17** plus delivery, required two different price approvals and returned **WooCommerce order #17**, **HK$46.00 captured by Stripe in test mode**, and **HK$0 real charge**. A separate merchant-side order read and two fresh Stripe reads matched. The simulated Visa ended in 4242. ## Merchant boundary

The installed core version’s `expected_total` rejects a higher calculated amount. A separate merchant test exercised a lower cart total at the final check and observed HTTP 409 before the gateway. Review of the installed Woo code found that it wraps exceptions raised at payment dispatch. The test-only merchant extension now preserves the exact revision and validates both the cart basis and the final order. A request-scoped, temporary fixture changed the shipping immediately at gateway dispatch. The adapter returned revised totals of HK$43 and HK$41 from HK$42, with no payment mapping. The temporary fixture used an unusable test method and was removed. These checks may materialize unpaid Woo draft/pending orders; the charge count distinguishes those from payments. An initial WP-CLI-only harness did not enter the actual gateway hook, so its response was excluded from the acceptance evidence.
