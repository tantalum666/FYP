2. Problem Statement & Project Objectives
Instructions: Describe the business or technical problem in information technology that this project intends to address. Define 2-3 specific project objectives (what your solution intends to achieve for the target users/businesses).
Problem Statement:Foodservice teams, schools and individuals need reliable links between food quantity, nutritional information and leftovers. Buffets need timely replenishment, schools need visibility of meal portions and food-group balance, and individuals need easier meal records.We propose a configurable system that continuously monitors food while serving containers or meal plates remain on sensing bases. Measured quantities, food recognition and reference nutrition data support replenishment, school meal review and personal nutrition awareness. Metafoodx and Controliza inform the design; the prototype will evaluate these uses through controlled scenarios.Project Objectives:1. Build a reusable IoT and vision prototype for continuous monitoring of food identity, quantity and leftovers in serving and individual-meal configurations.2. Provide AI-assisted replenishment and reference-based portion/nutrition feedback through foodservice, school and personal profiles, retaining human confirmation and uncertainty indicators.3. Evaluate measurement, recognition, nutrition calculations, replenishment outcomes and usability against manual references and simple baselines in controlled scenarios.
3. Proposed Project Scope & Key Features
Instructions: Define the scope of your system. List and briefly describe the key modules, functions, or features that your group plans to implement. Projects must be implementation-oriented and student-centered.
Key Subsystems / Features:A. Shared continuous sensing. Track weight, dish identity, refills and leftovers; handle tare, movement, occlusion and stale data. Record surface temperature where applicable.B. Nutrition estimates. Link measured portions to registered recipes or matched food-composition records. Separate food provided, leftovers and user-confirmed intake estimates; flag uncertain food/weight mappings.C. Three application profiles. Foodservice: replenishment timing and batch tasks. Schools: meal-portion/food-group review and aggregate leftovers. Personal: adult meal records and reference nutrition summaries.D. Interfaces and feedback. Bilingual dashboards, alerts and reports. Retain QR menus with live availability, completed-order popularity and estimated waits; basic ordering and kitchen status remain available in foodservice profiles.Prototype Scope: One shared six-month prototype, up to three weighing positions and 4-6 registered foods/recipes. Demonstrate three profiles using separately weighed items or known recipes and anonymous school example meals.Expected Users & Roles: Kitchen staff, school catering teams/educators and adult personal users; parents/students can view appropriate menu and educational summaries.
4. Technical Stack & Special Requirements
Instructions: Detail the hardware and software tools you intend to use. Highlight how your project incorporates contemporary technologies (AI, IoT, Cloud, etc.) and how it aligns with modern considerations like environmental-friendly design, bilingual/multi-language support, or cross-border deployability to meet Greater China business needs.
Development Technologies & Tools:• Hardware: Raspberry Pi 5, load cells with ADC interfaces, overhead camera, surface-temperature sensor and display/LEDs. Adapt supports for serving containers or meal plates; assess RGB-D by testing. Existing UNO R4 WiFi is optional.• Programming Languages: Python, TypeScript, HTML/CSS; C++ only if a microcontroller is used.• Frameworks / Libraries: OpenCV, PyTorch/scikit-learn, FastAPI, React and i18next.• Database / Storage: PostgreSQL, local logs, selected USDA FoodData Central records and registered recipes. Retain source IDs, units and cooking-state/recipe metadata.• Target Platforms: Local Linux device and responsive web interfaces with configurable scenario profiles.Special Project Considerations:• Contemporary Technologies: IoT sensing, AI food recognition and demand estimates, reference-based nutrition calculations and live feedback; optional cloud backup.• Environmental-Friendly Solution / Social Benefit: Support school meal review, personal nutrition awareness and avoidable-waste reduction with reusable hardware.• Bilingual / Multi-Language Support: English and Traditional Chinese interfaces.• Cross-Border Deployment / Greater China Needs: Configurable food catalogues, meal plans, currency and time zone; reviewed regional guidance, such as Hong Kong school-lunch guidelines.• Responsible Use: Educational estimates, not diagnosis or prescribed diets. Uncertain mixed meals require confirmation; school demonstrations use anonymous meals without child health profiles.• Feasibility / Validation: Test sensing and nutrition calculations against known portions/manual references; compare replenishment with fixed-threshold and consumption-rate baselines. Use controlled meals and adult usability tests; field access is not assumed. Full payment/ERP integration is excluded.
