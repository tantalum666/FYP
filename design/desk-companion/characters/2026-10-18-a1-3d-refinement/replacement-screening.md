The owner’s broader personal-reference allowance makes NC sources such as Galaxy-Chan eligible for further visual research, and the free personal tier of Muffin hair was actually inspected in the follow-up. Permissions to alter, input into AI, redistribute or use in a shared application remain the creator's separate conditions. No download/adoption is inferred from listing a source.

## Quality gate for the next attempt

Inspect the actual existing model in neutral light, front/three-quarter/profile, at face and full-body scale. Require a convincing adult anime face, readable eyelids/lips, coherent hair volume/strand texture, intact clothing topology and usable expressions before adaptation. The A1 reference remains the target. Obtain a small face/hair comparison before repeating whole-body procedural edits or announcing a new model in the app. Free licensing and a rendered thumbnail alone do not satisfy this gate.
