# Owner-reviewed WooCommerce shopping in Alice

An owner can ask for a basket in Alice’s regular composer, for example “two packs of pocket tissues and one stationery pack.” The linked WooCommerce test shop quotes in HKD for its synthetic Hong Kong customer. A virtual cart can have zero shipping. A request for real public prices continues through the existing research tools. [Single-product Simulator acceptance](../../output/qa/2026-11-19-alice-shopping-conversation/verification.md).

## Quote, confirmation and receipt

- The existing Session calls `sandbox_shop_quote` once with `{"items":[{"product":"FYP-QA-POCKET-TISSUES","quantity":2},{"product":"FYP-QA-DELIVERY-PACK","quantity":1}]}`. The original `{"product":"FYP-QA-DELIVERY-PACK","quantity":1}` form also works. Up to eight entries can share one review. Names and SKUs resolving to the same product or concrete variation combine, with a maximum of 99 selling units per SKU. A variable parent requires a specific variation SKU. Any ambiguous, missing or unavailable entry stops the whole quote.
- Woo prices one addressed guest cart and applies its selected delivery rate once. The merchant checks the delivery line and its tax against the order’s shipping amounts. Home shows one summary. Order details list the SKUs, quantities, unit prices, subtotals and adjustments. Approval covers each line’s gross, discount and tax, the individual fees, shipping rate and amount, currency and final total. Moving a charge between lines requires a new review even when the total stays the same.
- The owner can use the card or explicitly confirm its amount in the ordinary chat. The common review mechanism binds owner, login, conversation, task, review version and amount. Full access preserves this confirmation.
- The app rebuilds the cart at confirmation. If its commercial terms have changed, it displays a new review and retains the prior amount for comparison. The merchant also checks the actual order before dispatching to its payment gateway. Both increases and decreases require another owner decision. A checkout-only adjustment retains the cart from which it was calculated; the payment gateway has to see the approved final order.
- Each review has one merchant reference. The store serializes submissions and saves the reference to the Woo order before running the gateway. On a lost acknowledgement the app checks that reference. A retry can return the same order. A duplicate cannot authorize another charge for that review.
- The normal Woo Store API and the existing Payment Plugins for Stripe gateway perform payment. The desk verifies the Woo order’s items, shipping, paid status and total, then retrieves the linked Stripe PaymentIntent and Charge. The receipt shows the merchant order number and captured test amount after matching the account, test mode, currency, amount, capture, refund state and test Visa. A follow-up such as “帮我查回这笔购物订单的订单号和实际模拟扣款金额” rereads the merchant and Stripe. [WooCommerce Checkout API](https://developer.woocommerce.com/docs/apis/store-api/resources-endpoints/checkout/) · [Stripe test payments](https://docs.stripe.com/testing).

## Paid order cancellation

The FYP merchant accepts a buyer’s no-reason cancellation for a paid, shippable test order still in processing with no recorded dispatch or prior refund. In the original conversation the owner can ask whether cancellation is available, ask Alice to cancel and refund the order, or use the order-specific refund button. A generic request spanning multiple recorded orders requires a selection. Reopen the original payment on its card, or name its order number, for example “查回购物订单 #21 的退款结果”. The composer’s inferred price selection cannot authorize cancellation of an unrelated order. Fulfilled orders are outside this shop policy.

WooCommerce records the buyer request before contacting its refund gateway. Alice checks the original order, test account, PaymentIntent and Charge, then reads every page of Refunds for that charge. The receipt shows the original gross, the sum of distinct successful refunds, the remaining net and any pending amount. Pending funds stay in the net until settlement. Several successful partial refunds can complete the reversal once the Woo order is refunded and both ledgers agree on the full amount. [Ordinary cancellation and independent readback](../../output/qa/2026-11-21-commerce-aftercare/verification.md).

The home shows a short order result. Expand the receipt or use its original-payment control to check the amounts. An in-flight buyer cancellation stays under observation while the ledgers update. Conflicting records can require merchant review. The receipt shows corroborated amounts, marks unknown values as unverified and labels a retained prior reading as historical. For a pending refund the owner can say “退款到了告诉我”. The running desk follows the original payment under the existing login and proactivity settings. Reopening and background checks only read records. [Background refund replay](../../output/qa/2026-11-21-quiet-order-followup/verification.md).

In the local HK$33 fixture, HK$7 settled and HK$8 pending leave a HK$26 net charge. After restart, the same two Refunds and a third HK$18 settlement reconcile to HK$0 net with one original checkout and no cancellation request. Pre-existing receipts acquire their payment binding on the first compatible readback. [Partial and pending refund fixtures](../tests/test_shop_refund_aftercare.py).

## Installed test store

The provider is pinned to the existing loopback WooCommerce test store on port 8993. The store uses the FYP must-use plugin. The basket revision in this source tree has local fixture coverage. Previously paid single-product orders reopen using their original flat terms and merchant reference. Configure the plugin’s `fyp_alice_review_hash` option with the SHA-256 digest of a randomly generated private review key. It authenticates requests and requires the actual Woo gateway to be in test mode. The store publishes a fingerprint of its test publishable key for matching the operator’s Stripe sandbox. Payment details come from the documented Visa fixture. The application has no input for real payment or delivery details. [Woo extension](../integrations/woocommerce/fyp-reviewed-checkout.php)

Set `DESK_WOO_SANDBOX_CONFIG` on the authorized backend to an owner-only regular JSON file with `review_key`, `stripe_account` and `stripe_keys_path`. The private keys file contains the sandbox’s `test_publishable_key` and `test_secret_key`. The merchant option and local configuration must belong to the same installation. Keep the files and upstream payment identifiers outside Git. Shared review storage and UI polling are in the commerce modules; Woo order processing and flight fulfillment keep their own provider adapters. [Flight workflow](conversation-flight-booking.md).

The shipped example SKU is `FYP-QA-DELIVERY-PACK`. The sandbox’s HK delivery fixture is back at HK$8 after acceptance. The older virtual SKU and its previous guest order remain available. A re-read of a paid order uses its historical amounts.

The local basket fixture quotes two HK$4 tissue packs and a HK$17 stationery variation with HK$8 delivery, then verifies the HK$33 capture and full refund. A pending pre-basket quote gets a fresh review of its itemized terms before payment. Run `.venv/bin/pytest -q tests/test_shop_basket.py` from the desk directory. The direct merchant terms fixture also runs when a local PHP CLI is available. [Basket tests](../tests/test_shop_basket.py)
