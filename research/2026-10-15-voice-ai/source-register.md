## Access limitations and evidence excluded from new rankings

- Jarvis returned no readable body in this pass. Its 29 September snapshot remains historical; no fresh score or audio listening is claimed.
- The [EVA author page](https://servicenow.github.io/eva/) returned no readable leaderboard. Grok's quoted 74% was not independently revalidated. The discarded eva-bench guess is not a source.
- One OpenAI Audio-overview fetch returned HTTP403 challenge. Its contents were not used. Realtime/model/pricing/accounting pages succeeded; the component list uses the successful price table.
- StepFun .com failed in the web text fetch; its public HTTP HTML returned200 with the free Preview item, and the official .ai price page supplied readable current speech rows. No login or trial activation.
- Doubao numbered-page redirects and the 3.0 billing-SKU gap are retained. A general price or consumer demo is not an exact-model quote or integration receipt.
- Search aggregators, reposts and old consumer Grok3 stories were discovery leads only; technical conclusions use official pages and benchmark authors.
